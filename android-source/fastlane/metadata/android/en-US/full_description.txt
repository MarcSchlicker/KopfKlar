KopfKlar is a local headache diary without an account or cloud sync.

• Record headaches, intensity, duration and possible triggers
• Track medication effectiveness, pain before/after use and duration of relief
• Explore the calendar, filters, statistics and personal reports
• Export and restore JSON backups; export CSV and PDF reports
• Import DMKG diary PDFs locally
• Use optional reminders, cycle records and an app passcode
• German and English interface with light and dark appearance

Diary entries stay on your device. The passcode protects the interface; it does not encrypt stored data. Backups contain personal information. External support links open the browser only when requested.

KopfKlar is a documentation tool and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. It is not affiliated with DMKG.
