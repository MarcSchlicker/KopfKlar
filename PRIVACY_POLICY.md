# Privacy Policy – KopfKlar

Last updated: 31 August 2026

KopfKlar is a local-first headache diary. Your diary entries, medication,
cycle, therapy, trigger, and settings data are stored on your device.

## Data storage

- KopfKlar does not require an account.
- KopfKlar does not operate a cloud synchronisation service.
- KopfKlar does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- KopfKlar does not automatically transmit diary data to the developer,
  Google, or other third parties.

## Exports and backups

You decide when to create a PDF report or a data backup. Exports and backups
are created only after you request them and are shared only through the
Android sharing options you select. The recipient and any service used for
sharing are subject to their own privacy terms.

## Notifications

Local reminders are scheduled on your device. They do not send your diary data
to a server.

## External links

If you choose to open an external link from the app, your browser or the
selected app connects to that service under its own privacy policy.

## Contact

The current contact details, where applicable, are shown in the relevant app
store listing.
